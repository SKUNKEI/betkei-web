## Scope and operator

This policy applies to BetKei, including the website at [betkei.skunkei.com](https://betkei.skunkei.com/), related API requests, sports-market analysis, and Betfair account features.

BetKei is operated by **Keirran Blackley as an individual**, under the SKUNKEI platform and brand. Contact: [service@skunkei.com](mailto:service@skunkei.com).

BetKei currently requires an authenticated account with administrative authorization. Features and availability may change. Where applicable, information is handled under the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and Australian Privacy Principles, and other privacy laws that apply to you.

## Information handled

- **Account and access information:** account/profile details, authentication state, session information, access roles, and security records needed to operate and protect the service.
- **BetKei activity:** sports and leagues viewed, event and market identifiers, captured prices, prediction inputs and outputs, resolved results, and account-associated prediction records.
- **Betfair information:** if a Betfair connection is used, the username, password, and application key you submit for authentication; the resulting session token; and account information returned by Betfair, such as available balance, exposure, current orders, settled orders, and bet outcomes. The Betfair password is sent to Betfair for authentication and is not stored by BetKei. The application key and resulting session token are stored encrypted by the service.
- **Betting action records:** when a bet is placed or an unmatched bet is cancelled, the service may retain account-associated market/selection identifiers, requested odds and stake, provider response/status, matched amount, and timestamps for operation, display, reconciliation, and security.
- **Sports data:** event schedules, league/team names, scores, and historical results received from sports-data providers. These are used to provide event views and available form context.
- **Technical and support information:** IP address, browser/device details, requested page, referrer, request time, error/security records, and messages you send for support may be processed by the service or its infrastructure providers.
- **Website analytics:** Google Analytics may receive page-view, usage, and browser/device information when the website analytics integration is active. BetKei does not intentionally include Betfair passwords, application keys, session tokens, or bet-form credential fields in analytics events.

Do not send passwords, account keys, or other secrets in support requests or URLs.

## How information is used

Information is used to:

- Authenticate accounts and enforce access permissions.
- Retrieve and display events, markets, current prices, scores, results, and available historical form.
- Calculate and display model estimates and historical performance summaries.
- Connect to Betfair when requested, show account/order information, and submit or cancel an order only after an authorized user's action and confirmation.
- Record prediction and bet-action outcomes for display, reconciliation, security, and service operation.
- Measure website usage through Google Analytics, where enabled.
- Prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, protect users and the service, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Model outputs and projected profit/loss are informational calculations, not actual returns or promises. Actual Betfair order activity is distinct from model predictions and depends on Betfair's market, matching, and account status.

## Connected services

- **Betfair Exchange AU:** receives the Betfair credentials you submit for authentication and requests necessary to retrieve account/market data or perform an order action you have authorized. BetKei does not store the Betfair password; the Betfair application key and session token are encrypted at rest by the service. Betfair handles its own account and transaction data under its terms and privacy policy.
- **TheSportsDB:** receives server requests for supported fixtures, scores, leagues, or related historical sports information. BetKei uses returned data to populate event/result views and, where available, historical form. BetKei does not send your Betfair password or session token to TheSportsDB.
- **Google Analytics:** receives website usage and browser/device information when the configured analytics integration is active. Google's processing is governed by Google's privacy terms.
- **Hosting, email, and content providers:** infrastructure providers, account-message delivery services, and providers serving presentation assets may process technical information needed to deliver their services.

Availability, coverage, and provider behavior are controlled partly by those third parties. Connected services have their own terms and privacy policies.

## Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information may be processed by service providers as described above, or disclosed when required by law, in response to a valid legal request, or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or others. Providers may process information in locations subject to their own arrangements and policies.

## Storage and security

BetKei uses account authentication, role-based access checks, CSRF protection for state-changing browser requests, server-side validation, and HTTPS/TLS for supported production connections. Sensitive Betfair connection values (the application key and session token) are encrypted at rest by the service. BetKei's service can use those values to make authorized requests; BetKei is not a user-held-key or zero-knowledge service.

The Betfair account password is sent to Betfair for authentication and is not persisted by BetKei. Prediction, price, order, and audit records may be stored in service databases and operational systems. No online service can guarantee complete security. Protect your account and devices, and check Betfair directly if an order's status is uncertain.

## Retention and deletion

Account-associated records are retained as needed to provide BetKei, show prediction and betting history, maintain account integrity, investigate errors or abuse, and meet applicable obligations. Bet placement audit records and operational logs are subject to service retention and cleanup practices; exact periods may vary by record type and service configuration. Temporary market-price and sports-data caches are retained only to support timely service responses.

When an account is deleted, account-associated records may be removed under the platform's account-deletion process, subject to records that must be retained for security, reconciliation, or legal obligations. Infrastructure and provider logs follow their respective retention practices. To ask about or request deletion of information, contact [service@skunkei.com](mailto:service@skunkei.com).

## Your choices and rights

Use BetKei only with an account and connected Betfair permissions you are authorized to use. You can stop using the service and may revoke connected access through the relevant provider or request assistance from SKUNKEI. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, request a copy of, restrict, object to, or request deletion of personal information.

Send requests to [service@skunkei.com](mailto:service@skunkei.com), identifying BetKei and the account involved. We may need to verify your identity. Do not email passwords, application keys, session tokens, or other access secrets.

## Age and responsible use

BetKei is not intended for children. Use betting features only where legal and only if you meet the applicable minimum age, including 18+ where required. BetKei provides statistics, estimates, and exchange tools for analysis and entertainment. It is not betting advice, financial advice, or a guarantee of results. Betting carries a risk of loss; set limits and do not chase losses.
